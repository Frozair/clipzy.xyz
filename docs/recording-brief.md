# Screen recording brief — Shipaton promo

Nine short recordings. Record each one separately; do not attempt one perfect
run. Short clips give the edit control over timing, and a fluffed take costs you
fifteen seconds instead of two minutes.

Drop the files in `~/Desktop/clipzy-recordings/` using the names below.

## Before you start

- **Close every other browser tab.** The Twitch sign-in hands off to Chrome for
  about half a second and the screen recorder catches the address bar. That is
  how your own tabs ended up in the last two videos.
- **Do Not Disturb on.** A notification banner kills a take.
- **Feed fit set to Height**, the toggle top right of the Feed. It silently
  reverts to Width, which letterboxes the clip into a thin strip.
- **Use clips you own.** Your software and dev streams, not the Resident Evil
  footage. Shipaton rules bar copyrighted material without permission, and your
  own streams are also brighter, which was the other complaint.
- **Pick one clip and follow it** through recordings 3 to 9. The video reads as
  one clip going from the deck to a finished vertical video, so it must be the
  same clip throughout. Ideally one where your face-cam is clearly visible.
- Android's built-in recorder is fine. No audio needed, we strip it.

## Things that ruin a take

- **Never show the share sheet.** It appears on its own after an export and
  lists real contacts by name and photo. Stop recording before it opens.
- **Do not tap an ad.** There is an inline ad card in the library grid and a
  banner in the editor. One of them opened another app entirely last time.
- Avoid the very edges of the screen when dragging. The trim handles sit inside
  Android's back-gesture zone, so dragging the end handle inward exits the
  editor. Start the drag slightly downward, then sideways.

## The recordings

| # | File | Length | What to do |
|---|------|--------|------------|
| 1 | `01-signin.mp4` | ~10s | Start signed out on the "Your best Twitch moments, sorted." screen. Hold 2s, tap **Sign in with Twitch**, let the deck load, let the first clip play a few seconds. |
| 2 | `02-review.mp4` | ~15s | On the deck with a clip playing. Swipe right to keep. Let the next play ~2s. Swipe left to archive. Let the next play ~2s. Tap **Undo**. Hold 2s. |
| 3 | `03-library.mp4` | ~12s | Open **Library**. Hold on the grid 3s. Scroll down slowly, scroll back. Tap the clip you are following. |
| 4 | `04-download.mp4` | ~12s | Clip details, still a cloud clip. Hold 2s on "Download once to unlock editing", tap **Download**, let it reach "Ready to edit", hold 3s. |
| 5 | `05-format.mp4` | ~12s | Tap **Edit & export**. Let the editor load. Tap the **Vertical social 9:16** header, hold on the sheet 3s so all three formats read, pick Vertical social. |
| 6 | `06-stacked.mp4` | ~20s | **The important one.** Tap **Layout**, hold 2s on Full vs Stacked, pick **Stacked**. Then fix the framing: zoom and drag the top half until your face-cam is properly centred, not off to the side. Then set the bottom half on the gameplay. Tap **Done**. Go slowly, this is the beat that sells the product. |
| 7 | `07-overlays.mp4` | ~12s | Tap the overlay chip at the top right of the preview. It cycles No overlay → TikTok → Shorts → Reels. Pause ~2.5s on each so each one reads. |
| 8 | `08-captions.mp4` | ~25s | Tap **Generate captions**. Let it run (we cut the wait). When the caption editor appears, cycle the styles: Bold, Classic, Minimal. Press play so the captions run over the video for a few seconds. |
| 9 | `09-export.mp4` | ~15s | Tap **Export**. Let the progress run to completion. **Stop recording the moment it finishes**, before the share sheet slides up. |

## Optional tenth

If the Play purchase flow gets fixed, `10-pro.mp4`, about 15s: the paywall, tap
to subscribe, the purchase completing, and the UI switching to Pro. Judges like
seeing the money path work. Right now the billing sheet closes instantly with no
error, so there is nothing to film.

## If a take goes wrong

Re-record only that one. Nothing here depends on the others except that
recordings 3 to 9 follow the same clip.
