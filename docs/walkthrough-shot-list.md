# Clipzy walkthrough — shot list (v2)

Written after reviewing v1. The problems with v1, in Frozair's words: the
clips aren't gaming, "I'm off to the side" in the edit, "we don't really go
through some of the buttons," the vertical overlays are described but never
tapped through, and "the video doesn't look cohesive."

Every control below was read off the live screen, so the coordinates and
labels are real.

## The cohesion rule

v1 was a tour of screens. v2 follows **one clip** from the deck to a finished
vertical video. Same clip, start to finish. Nothing is shown that doesn't
happen to that clip.

## Shots

### 1. Connect (~6s)
Sign-in screen → Twitch auth → deck loads. Unchanged from v1; this beat worked.

### 2. Review (~12s)
Swipe through **gaming clips**. Keep, archive, keep. End on the clip we follow
for the rest of the video, and keep it.

Set the feed fit to **Height** before recording. It reverts to Width, which
letterboxes the clip into a thin strip. Check it in the first frame of every
take.

### 3. Library + find it again (~10s)
The kept clip is in the library. Sort, filter, then search for it by name.
Shorter than v1: this beat ran 22s and earned about 10.

### 4. Download (~8s)
Open it. "Cloud clip → Download once to unlock editing." Tap Download, watch
the status flip to "Ready to edit."

### 5. Open the editor, set the format (~10s)
Tap Edit & export. Then tap the **"Vertical social 9:16"** header and show the
sheet: Vertical social 9:16, Square 1:1, Landscape 16:9. Pick Vertical social.
Never shown in v1.

### 6. Layout, then actually reframe (~16s)
Tap **Layout** → Stacked. The face-cam sits in the source's bottom-left corner,
so the default stacked crop puts Frozair off to the side. **This is the beat
that fixes his note.** Drag the top crop until the face-cam is centred, then
drag the bottom crop onto the gameplay. The whole point of the feature is
visible only when you see it corrected.

### 7. Tap through the platform overlays (~10s)
Tap the overlay chip at the top right of the preview. It cycles:
**No overlay → TikTok → Shorts → Reels.** Land on the one that best shows the
safe zone covering something. Never shown in v1, and the site copy promises it.

### 8. Trim (~6s)
Tap **Trim** and drag a clip handle in. v1 opened on Trim without using it.

### 9. Captions (~18s)
Tap **Captions** → Generate captions. Progress runs on-device. Then fix a word,
and cycle the styles: Bold, Classic, Minimal, Top.

### 10. Export (~8s)
Tap **Export** and let it render. v1 stopped before this, so the narration had
to claim it rather than show it. Uses one of the 20 monthly free exports.

Target: about 105 seconds, up from 83.

## Optional 11th shot

**More → "Edit with more context"** pulls earlier stream context from the Twitch
VOD, up to 60s. The site mentions it; no video has ever shown it. Worth adding
if the beat reads quickly.

## Capture settings

- Status bar: systemui demo mode, 9:41, 100%, wifi full. DND on.
- Feed fit: **Height**, verified per take.
- Ads: mask only where an ad is actually on screen. In v1 a blanket mask clipped
  the Layout sheet. There are now three ad surfaces: the editor banner, a
  full-card inline ad in the library grid, and an interstitial that can open
  another installed app if tapped. Keep gestures away from all three.
- Keep the full frame height. Cropping the bottom removes the tab bar and the
  navigation becomes invisible.

## Open question

The only gaming clips currently on the phone are from one dark horror game and
average 11.5/255 luminance against 68.9 for the footage shipped today, six times
darker. They read as a black rectangle at phone-frame size on the page. Shots 2
and 6 need brighter gameplay to be worth re-recording.
