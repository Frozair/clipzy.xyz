CLIPZY WALKTHROUGH — READ THIS ALOUD
====================================

One take is fine. Don't watch the video while you read and don't try to hit
any timings — I'll align it afterwards.

The only thing that matters: pause for about one second at each [PAUSE] so I
can find the boundaries cleanly. A visible breath is better than a tight edit.

If you fluff a line, just stop, pause, and say the whole line again. I'll keep
the better take.

------------------------------------------------------------------

This is Clipzy. You sign in with Twitch, and your own clips load
automatically.

                              [PAUSE]

Newest first, so you start with last night. Watch one, keep it or archive it,
and the next one's already playing.

                              [PAUSE]

Everything you keep lands in your library. Sort by newest, oldest, or view
count. Filter by what you've already exported.

                              [PAUSE]

If you remember a moment but not when it happened, just search for it.

                              [PAUSE]

Open one up. At this point it's still on Twitch, not on your phone.

                              [PAUSE]

One tap pulls it down. Now it's yours offline, and editing is unlocked.
That's a one-time thing per clip.

                              [PAUSE]

Here's the editor. Your clip is horizontal, TikTok and Shorts are vertical,
so something has to give.

                              [PAUSE]

Full drops the whole frame in. Stacked splits it, so your face-cam goes up
top and the gameplay sits underneath.

                              [PAUSE]

Captions come from the clip's own audio, generated right here on the phone.

                              [PAUSE]

Then fix anything it misheard, retime it, pick a style, and burn them in.
After that you export, and it's in your camera roll ready to post.
