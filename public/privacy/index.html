Clipzy

Privacy Policy

Effective July 30, 2026

Clipzy is local-first. The mobile app has no Clipzy account server, does not proxy your videos, and does not sell personal data.

What the mobile app processes

When you sign in, Clipzy connects directly to Twitch using Twitch OAuth. It processes your Twitch user ID, display name, profile image, access token, and information about clips from your channel so the app can show and manage your clip backlog.

Your access token is stored in encrypted Android storage or the iOS Keychain. Clip metadata, review decisions, collections, download records, edit settings, and export allowance are stored locally on your device and separated by Twitch account.

Videos, downloads, and exports

Playback and downloads come directly from Twitch. Editing and export happen on your device. Clipzy does not upload, host, or proxy your video files.

Automatic captions

Caption generation is optional. On Android, Clipzy supplies the clip’s audio to an on-device speech model; Android requires microphone permission for this speech API even though Clipzy does not record your microphone. On iOS, Clipzy requests Speech Recognition access and prefers on-device recognition when the device supports it. Apple may process speech through its service when on-device recognition is not available.

Website and Pro waitlist

The Clipzy website uses Vercel Analytics for aggregate site usage. If you join the optional Pro waitlist, the website sends your email address and basic signup attribution to Formspark for storage and Resend to deliver the confirmation email. This waitlist is separate from the mobile app.

Sharing and retention

Clipzy shares data with Twitch and the platform services described above only as needed to provide the feature you choose. Local app data remains until you delete it in Clipzy, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Waitlist emails are retained until they are no longer needed for launch communication or you ask us to remove them.

Your choices

You can sign out, revoke Clipzy in your Twitch connections, deny optional permissions, delete the active account’s local data from the app, or request deletion of a waitlist email.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@clipzy.xyz.
